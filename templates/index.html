About the Constitution

The supreme law of Zimbabwe adopted on 22 May 2013.

National Legal Framework

The Constitution of Zimbabwe Amendment (No. 20) Act, 2013 was approved overwhelmingly by the people of Zimbabwe in a national referendum on 16 March 2013 and signed into law on 22 May 2013. It establishes Zimbabwe as a unitary, democratic, and sovereign republic founded on the rule of law, separation of powers, and respect for human rights.
